A little world I kept for me.

Not a portfolio. Not a traditional birthday page. Just a tiny chaotic memory box for the girl who survived 19, built things anyway, laughed at bugs, changed languages mid-sentence, and somehow reached 20.

Before 19… there was 17.

I was learning Turkish by whispering. Fısıltıyla başladım. Literally. Quiet girl, tiny voice, giant dream. Çok mantıklı. 😂

I didn't know that a quiet little language journey would become Language Whispers. I just knew I wanted to speak a language that once felt impossible. O zamanlar çok korkmuştum ama yine de devam ettim.

const me = {

while (!thereYet) {

Very professional strategy. Would not recommend. 😂 Ama işe yaradı.

Then 18 came. I built Language Whispers. My first fee was 300 birr. Three hundred. 😭 Üç yüz lira değil, üç yüz birr. Ama kalbimde milyon gibiydi.

Someone had actually trusted something I built. Ben yaptım. And suddenly the whisper had a little microphone.

Then 19 happened.

I had dreamed of becoming a doctor for years. I prepared for it. Then I failed the Ethiopian entrance exam.

404: DREAM NOT FOUND

expected: "doctor"
received: "??????"

system: please remain calm
me: absolutely not 😭

I felt alone. I felt like I had lost myself. I was assigned to an Axum University remedial programme, but I couldn't make myself go back and start again. My brain basically said: “non merci.” 😭

O zamanlar çok korkmuştum. Gerçekten.

At 17 I had told myself, “If I fall, my dream will get me back up.” But when I actually fell, getting back up was not automatic. Turns out motivational quotes do not physically pick you up from the floor. Who knew? 😂

Je ne savais plus quoi faire de moi.

And still… j'ai continué. Not elegantly. Not with a five-year plan. Just… one tiny step. Bir adım daha.

if (lifeFallsApart) {
    breathe();
    cry();
    laughAtTheSituation();
    buildAgain();
}

College? Character development. 😭

I looked at nursing, IT, tour guide college… my brain opened 47 tabs and forgot which one was playing music. Ne yapacağım ben? 😂

I joined the tour guide programme, paid the fee, and then realized: this isn't me. Bu ben değilim.

Leaving was hard. But staying somewhere only because I was afraid to leave felt worse. Kendime ihanet etmek istemedim. I didn't have the perfect answer; I just knew that pretending was not the answer.

Me choosing a career: “This looks reasonable.”
Me five minutes later: “Who authorized this decision?” 😂
Moi: “Bon… on recommence.”

My first 10,000 birr. It had felt impossible. Then there it was.

“Attends… c'est vraiment à moi?”

I used my own money to pay college fees. I bought clothes and shoes. Ordinary things, maybe. But to me they were proof that I could create something and earn from it.

The laptop chapter.

I waited a long time for that laptop. Buying it was hard. My brother helped with the purchase, and that day became one of those days I will remember. I had sacrificed a lot of my own happiness just to get there. Kendimi yeniden ayağa kaldırmak zorundaydım.

I didn't just buy a laptop. I bought a door. Bir kapı açtım. And yes, I stared at it like: “Okay… now what?” 😂

Then I started programming. And apparently one project was not enough. 😭

Language Whispers. The café project. NURIVA. Debugging. More debugging. Debugging the debugging.

The story didn't end. It just changed programming languages. 😂

OPEN WHEN YOU FORGET WHO YOU ARE

Dear future me…

So we… somehow survived all that. 😭

Hi girl. If you're reading this, first of all: look at you. You made it past 20. Tebrikler kızım. 🎉

I hope you're laughing right now because I am writing this with absolutely no idea what your life looks like. Je n'ai aucune idée de ce que tu es devenue. Maybe you're building something huge. Maybe you're learning another language because apparently three was not enough. 😂

Please remember the girl who whispered Turkish because she was too shy to speak loudly. Fısıltıyla başladım. Remember the girl who failed a dream she thought was her whole identity and thought, for a while, that everything was over.

Remember the 10K. The college fees. The shoes. The laptop. The nights of debugging when the computer was basically saying, 404: patience not found. 😂

Remember how hard it was to buy that laptop. Remember that you didn't magically become brave. You just kept moving while being scared. Korkuyordum ama devam ettim.

If you're rich, successful and dramatic now, please stay humble. 😭 If you're still figuring things out, sakin ol. You were never supposed to have the entire map at 20.

And if you ever feel like you're late, read this sentence twice:

J'ai continué.
Devam ettim.
I kept going.

Don't become so busy becoming “future me” that you forget to be grateful for “this me.” She fought for you.

Now go drink water, fix your posture, open your laptop, and build something. 😂

Love,
you — at 19, almost 20. 🤍
